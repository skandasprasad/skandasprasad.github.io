Hierarchical Planning with H-JEPA World Models

hierarchical JEPA world models for planning and reasoning

Research project at New York University with Prof. Yann LeCun and Prof. Jean Ponce.

  • Researching hierarchical JEPA world models for hierarchical planning and reasoning.
  • Developing dynamic temporal abstraction methods that learn when low-level transitions should be grouped into higher-level states and actions.
  • Evaluating hierarchical latent planning on robotic manipulation environments.

Status: in progress.
