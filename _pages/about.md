I am a master's student in Computer Science at the [NYU Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://cs.nyu.edu/home/index.html).
My work is centered on representation learning, world models, computer vision, and multimodal machine learning, with a current focus on JEPA-style predictive objectives for planning and scientific data.

At NYU, I am researching hierarchical JEPA world models for planning and reasoning with Prof. Yann LeCun and Prof. Jean Ponce.
I am also working on multimodal protein retrieval from amino-acid sequences and 3D cryo-EM density maps with Prof. David Fouhey, and previously built active-learning pipelines for non-stationary Gaussian Processes with Prof. Andrew Gordon Wilson.

Before NYU, I completed a B.Tech. in Computer Science and Engineering at PES University and worked across AI research, generative AI, and computer vision internships at Bosch in Bengaluru.
That work included weather-resilient ADAS perception research, production-line computer vision, and a generative-AI information retrieval assistant.

Research interests:

- Hierarchical planning and world models
- Self-supervised and multimodal representation learning
- Computer vision for robotics and autonomous systems
- Gaussian Processes and uncertainty-aware active learning
- Low-resource and code-mixed language modeling
