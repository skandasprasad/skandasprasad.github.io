Research project at New York University with Prof. Andrew Gordon Wilson.

- Built an active-learning pipeline for Gaussian Processes with Generalized Spectral Mixture kernels.
- Used ensemble disagreement to guide sampling in non-stationary environments.
- Compared Monte Carlo epistemic-variance acquisition with BALD and other baselines.
- Analyzed structural uncertainty, sampling bias, and predictive performance.

Status: completed in December 2025.
