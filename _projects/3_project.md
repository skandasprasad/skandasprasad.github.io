Course research project at New York University with Prof. Yann LeCun and Prof. Alfredo Canziani.

- Built global, spatial, and multiscale JEPA architectures under a 100M-parameter constraint.
- Evaluated frozen representations using linear and KNN probes.
- Placed second on the course leaderboard and presented the work as an Outstanding Project.

Status: completed in April 2026.
