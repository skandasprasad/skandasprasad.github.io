Research project at New York University with Prof. David Fouhey.

- Developing a multimodal representation framework that jointly embeds amino-acid sequences and 3D cryo-EM density maps.
- Using a JEPA-style cross-modal predictive objective for sequence-structure retrieval.
- Targeting weakly aligned biological data where exact sequence-structure supervision is limited.

Status: in progress.
